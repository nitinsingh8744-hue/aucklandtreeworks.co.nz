    Ask us in chat
    Do you offer free quotes?

    Yes. Every job starts with a free quote. Call us on 021 635 888, book online or use the chat on this page.

    Do I need Council consent to remove or prune my tree?

    It depends. Auckland's tree rules changed in 2013, so your tree may have no protection – or it may need consent before work starts. We work closely with Auckland Council planning teams and can advise you as part of your free quote.

    Are you insured and qualified?

    Yes. Auckland Tree Works is fully insured with liability insurance, Site Wise registered & approved, and a member of the New Zealand Arboricultural Association. Our lead arborist Eli has over 20 years of experience.

    Can you remove trees in tight or difficult spaces?

    Yes. We specialise in confined and difficult sites, and we use cranes for trees that require it – always working to industry best practice.

    Can you grind the stump after a tree is removed?

    Yes. We can grind stumps straight after a removal, or come back for an old stump left from a previous job.

    What happens to the wood and green waste?

    We aim to be environmentally sustainable. Where possible, timber is cut into firewood and the mulch is made available for gardens.

    Do you work with builders and developers?

    Yes. We regularly work with construction companies and property developers on site clearances, arboricultural reports and supervision of works around trees.
